Background

About

I got into security in middle school through a web pentest course by Eng. Ebraheem Hegazy. I didn't know much at the time, I just wanted to understand how things break. From there I worked through Cybrary, Udemy, HackThisSite and CTF platforms, and eventually settled on Hack The Box.

HTB is where most of my practical skills come from. I've rooted 250+ machines, reached Master rank, and finished the CPTS path, which covers the full chain from recon to domain compromise. Bug bounty hunting has been a parallel track since 2019.

At ExploitUs (July to August 2026) I did vulnerability research and wrote specifications for web and mobile pentest labs. Before that, a GRC internship at Vosyn AI gave me hands-on time with ISO 27001, NIST and risk assessments, so I understand the defensive and compliance side as well.

I'm looking for a junior pentesting role where I can keep learning on real engagements. I write up what I learn on this site.

NetProbe

Python  •  Ongoing

An async, modular port scanner and service fingerprinting tool. It supports TCP connect and SYN scans and UDP scans, runs a multi-stage service and version detection pipeline, inspects TLS certificates, and has adaptive timing templates with rate limiting.
